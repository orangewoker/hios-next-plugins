# Pose editing attribution

Reuses the AnalyticIKSolver and reach clamp from web/pose_studio/core.js in https://github.com/xiaozhuguang/ComfyUI-xiaozhuguang at commit 1fbc82397cf231ea0ed2a28e0e45e817b9d6e0e2. Source lineage: AHEKOT/ComfyUI_VNCCS_Utils → XZG → ComfyUI-xiaozhuguang.

The extracted solver is unmodified. HIOS adds a 16-joint mapping, reachable-target guards, direct screen-plane dragging, history and integration with its existing document/export pipeline. No MakeHuman assets, SAM/MHR models, pose-library assets or ComfyUI backend are bundled.
